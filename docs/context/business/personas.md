# Personas

Three personas, built only from `docs/discovery/`. First names are the ones used in the stakeholder notes; all people are fictional.

## Lucía, the resident

- **Context:** Lives in block 7 with two children. Often has relatives and deliveries visiting.
- **Goals:** Create a pass in under a minute from her phone. Send it by WhatsApp as a link. Create passes for one visit or for a weekend.
- **Frustrations:** Calling the gate. Explaining a picture to a guest. Having a relative's full name stored in a system.
- **Uses the product to:** Fill in the pass form, copy the verify link, and share it.

## Efraín, the guard

- **Context:** Night shift at the main gate, in a booth. Uses an older Android phone with a small, sometimes cracked screen. Signal comes and goes, mostly in rain.
- **Goals:** Decide in seconds. See a big, obvious green or red answer.
- **Frustrations:** Cameras and scanning that take a minute. Small text at 3 am. Not being sure who is allowed.
- **Uses the product to:** Open the pass the visitor shows and read the result. He prefers typing a short code to scanning (see open questions in the PRD).

## Marisol, the administrator

- **Context:** Works in the office on weekdays. Handles complaints and keeps the list of people who are banned.
- **Goals:** A modern look the board will approve. A monthly entries report that opens in a spreadsheet. A blocklist that always works.
- **Frustrations:** Searching the paper notebook. Answering for incidents without records.
- **Uses the product to:** Review the entry log, export it as CSV, and maintain the blocklist data.

## Open questions

- Do guards or the administrator revoke passes? The discovery material does not say.
- Does the administrator need to see passes that were created but never used? Not stated.
