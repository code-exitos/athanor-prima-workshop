# Product vision: Neighborhood Access

## Vision

Every visitor to Tartan Fields arrives with a pass their resident created, and every guard can trust a clear green or red answer at the gate.

## Value proposition

| For | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Residents | Create and share a pass in under a minute, without calling the gate. |
| Guards | One clear answer on a phone screen, with a reason when access is denied. |
| The board | A searchable record of entries and refusals, and a blocklist that always wins. |

## How it works, in one paragraph

A resident fills in a short form. The app creates a pass and signs it so it cannot be forged. The pass is shown as a QR code and a link. The guard opens the link, and the app shows access granted or access denied. The first version needs no server: everything runs in the browser.

## Principles

- **Simple first.** Fewer screens, fewer words, big results.
- **Private by default.** Visitors are identified by an alias, never a legal name.
- **Safe at the gate.** When in doubt, the answer is deny, and the reason is shown.
- **Cheap to run.** Static hosting only, until the board decides otherwise.

## Out of scope for now

A server, accounts, recurring passes, and notifications.
