# Kickoff brief: visitor access at Residencial Los Pinos

## Who we are talking to

The board (*junta directiva*) of Residencial Los Pinos, a gated community of 120 homes in 12 blocks. The board has three members. The administrator handles day-to-day work.

## What happens today

- Guards write every visitor in a paper notebook at the gate.
- A guard phones the resident to ask "Are you expecting someone?" The guard often cannot reach the resident.
- Nobody can search the notebook. After an incident last year, the board spent two days reading pages.

## What the board wants

1. Residents send their visitors a pass before the visit.
2. The guard checks the pass at the gate and lets the visitor in, or not.
3. The board can see who came in and when.
4. The board can ban people. A banned person must never get in.

## How they described the solution

> "It has to be **simple**. Our guards are not technical, and we do not have an IT person."

> "We do not want to pay for a server at launch. If it works, we may think about one later."

## What the board did not discuss

The board did not talk about phone brands, internet at the gate, or who else (besides residents) might need passes. These topics came up later in side conversations. See `stakeholder-notes.md`.

## Success, in the board's words

"In three months, the notebook is gone, and any guard can tell in a few seconds if a visitor is allowed."
