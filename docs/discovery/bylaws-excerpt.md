# Community Bylaws, Chapter 4: Visitors (excerpt)

*This is a fictional excerpt written for the workshop. It is not a real legal document.*

**Art. 18. Visitor pass.** A resident may authorize a visitor to enter the community by issuing a visitor pass for one housing unit. A visitor pass is valid from the start date until the end date that the resident chooses.

**Art. 19. Types of pass.** A pass is either for a single entry or for a period of days. A single-entry pass is used up the first time the visitor is admitted.

**Art. 20. Limit per unit.** A unit may have no more than 5 active passes at the same time. The board may change this number in an assembly.

**Art. 21. Identification of visitors.** The community keeps only the information it needs to identify a visit. The resident chooses how to name the visitor. The community does not require a legal name.

**Art. 22. Revocation.** A pass may be revoked when there is a reason to do so. A revoked pass gives no right of entry.

**Art. 23. Domestic staff.** Domestic staff are subject to the rules approved by the board.

**Art. 24. Blocklist.** The board keeps a list of persons who may not enter the community. A person on this list may not enter under any circumstances. This rule applies even when the person holds a valid visitor pass, and no resident may authorize an exception.

**Art. 25. Records.** The community keeps a record of each entry and each refused entry. Records are kept for 12 months.

**Art. 26. Guards.** The guard on duty applies this chapter at the gate and records the result.
