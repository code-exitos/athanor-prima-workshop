# Visitor Access Policy, Section 4: Visitors (excerpt)

*This is a fictional excerpt written for the workshop. It is not a real policy. The company name is used for illustration only; the people and rules are invented.*

**4.1 Visitor pass.** An administrator may authorize a visitor to enter a building by issuing a visitor pass for one building. A visitor pass is valid from the start date until the end date that the administrator chooses.

**4.2 Buildings.** The campus has four buildings: Main Tower, East Wing, West Wing and Annex. A pass is for one building.

**4.3 Identification of visitors.** The company keeps only the information it needs to identify a visit: the visitor's name as the administrator entered it, the building and the dates.

**4.4 Revocation.** A pass may be revoked when there is a reason to do so. A revoked pass gives no right of entry.

**4.5 Blocklist.** Security keeps a list of persons who may not enter any building. A person on this list may not enter under any circumstances. This rule applies even when the person holds a valid visitor pass, and no one may authorize an exception.

**4.6 Records.** The company keeps a record of each entry and each refused entry. Records are kept for 12 months.
